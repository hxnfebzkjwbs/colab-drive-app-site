Research workflow

Colab&Drive

Use Google Drive files in your Google Colab research notebooks and save the results back to Drive.

在 Google Colab 中读取 Google Drive 上的研究数据，并将生成的结果写回 Drive。

How it works

  1. Authorize the desktop OAuth client to access your Drive account.
  2. Run a research job in Colab. The job reads files from the Drive folders configured for that run and processes them in the Colab runtime.
  3. Save the output files to the Drive folder configured for the job.

Why the app asks for Drive access

The workflow reads existing research files and writes output files in Drive. The permission displayed by Google covers all Drive files; the research job uses the folders configured for that run. This informational website does not ask for Google authorization.
