Colab&Drive

Terms of use

Colab&Drive is an independent research workflow that runs in Google Colab and reads and writes files in Google Drive.

Run jobs only on files you are authorized to use. Before running a job, review its notebook code and its input and output folders. Keep OAuth credentials private.

Google Drive and Colab are Google services and are subject to Google’s terms. For details about data handling and revoking access, see the privacy policy.
